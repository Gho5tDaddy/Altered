✦ What Altered is

Altered is a private, rules-aware 2024 character sheet built around forms and transformations. It keeps your character, active form, resources, rolls, and conditions together during play.

◎ What the app is for

Use Altered during play to see the statistics, actions, spells, defenses, resources, and rule changes that apply to your current form. It supports the character sheet; it does not replace the DM or invent missing rules.

◈ Supported transformation types

Altered supports Base Form, Wild Shape, Polymorph, True Polymorph, Shapechange, Animal Shapes, additive overlays, and validated private custom transformations. The active card explains which statistics are retained.

➜ Getting Started
  1. Choose or import a character.
  2. Choose a form and check its status chips.
  3. Transform, then use the Actions, Spells, and Features tabs.
  4. Press New Turn when Action or Bonus Action counters are spent.
♙ Loading characters

Use the Character selector near the top. Switching characters safely creates that character’s own combat state and returns the sheet to Base Form.

⇩ Importing characters

Open Import & Content. For D&D Beyond, temporarily make the character Public, paste its link or ID, and review the result. If Needs Setup appears, use your authorized character page to enter only short playable mechanics; they remain private on this device and reapply automatically.

⌕ Browsing forms, search, and filters

Type in Search forms to match a form name or source. Use Filter for available, Wild Shape, spell, additive, or private forms. Locked choices stay visible in the full list so their requirements can be understood.

✧ Activating forms

Select a form, read the status chips and short reason, then press Transform or Activate. The app spends the correct action and resource only after all requirements pass.

↶ Ending forms and returning to normal

Press End Form beside the form selector. If it is disabled, its tooltip and status explain the missing Bonus Action or external ending condition. Base Form returns automatically when a rule ends the form.

◇ Tracking resources and turns

Green availability chips show what remains this turn. Resource chips show current/maximum uses. Active effects summarize ongoing changes. New Turn refreshes turn actions; rests recover only what the 2024 rule allows.

▣ Images

Upload artwork for the current character or form. Altered crops without distortion, optimizes the image locally, and stores it only on this device. Reset Art restores the built-in portrait.

⚙ Settings

Settings controls the magical form aura and reduced motion. It also shows the verified rules packs and legal SRD support-catalog status.

△ Troubleshooting
  • Can’t transform? Check the selected status and action/resource chips.
  • Spell unavailable? Check Rage, form spellcasting, Action, and spell slots.
  • Import blocked? Confirm the character is Public and uses 2024—not Legacy—rules.
  • Looks out of date? Reload once while the local server is running.
? FAQ

Does it save? Yes, locally. Does it work offline? The installed PWA keeps its validated built-in rules. Does it copy paid books? No; Needs Setup stores only your short private mechanical confirmations. Can I use a phone? Yes; use the hosted link from any current phone browser.

A About

Altered is a local-first personal utility for D&D 5e 2024 transformation play. It separates public SRD rules, private owned content, imported character facts, and mutable combat state to keep calculations explainable and private.
